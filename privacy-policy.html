DADA FANTER – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DADA FANTER Android application handles information. DADA FANTER provides photos, videos, and creative prompts for users.

1. Photos and videos provided by users

DADA FANTER does not provide the app developer with access to personal photos or videos that a user chooses on their device and submits directly to another app or service. If a user chooses to share a photo or prompt with Google Gemini, Google Flow, or another external service, that information is handled by the selected service under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review that service's privacy information before sharing personal content.

2. App content and online services

The app may connect to online services, including Firebase, to display or manage app content such as prompts and related media. Those services may process technical information needed to provide, secure, and operate their services. Their handling of information is governed by the providers' applicable terms and privacy documentation.

3. Information we receive

The developer does not receive users' personal photos or videos merely because users view content in DADA FANTER. Do not submit private or sensitive information through any feature unless you understand where it will be sent and how the receiving service handles it.

4. Third-party services

DADA FANTER may open or link to third-party services, including Google Gemini and Google Flow. These services are independent of DADA FANTER. Their privacy practices are described in their own policies. DADA FANTER does not control how third-party services collect or use information.

5. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the app content and services under our control. Information handled by third-party providers is subject to their security, retention, and deletion practices.

6. Children's privacy

DADA FANTER is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer, contact us through the developer contact details on the app's distribution page.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the DADA FANTER developer through the developer contact details provided on the app's distribution page.
